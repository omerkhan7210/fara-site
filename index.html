Good questions

What does SRS mean if I am selling a long-time home?

Seniors Real Estate Specialist training covers the decisions that come with moving after many years: timing, family involvement, right-sizing and the practical details. Fara works through them at your pace.

What does a buyer's representative do?

The ABR designation is about being on the buyer's side of the table through the search, the offer and the close, with opinions you can rely on.

What is CREN, in practice?

It is Fara's negotiation designation, and the reason offers and counteroffers get a plan before they get a reply.

Can you help me sell and buy at the same time?

Yes. The two timelines are planned together so one does not leave you stranded by the other.

What happens in the first conversation?

You describe the move, Fara asks questions, and you leave with a rough plan and timeline.
